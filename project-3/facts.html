The Tradeoff

Higher payout doesn't mean better.

Artists optimize for total revenue (streams × rate), not payout per stream.

  • Spotify: Reach + discovery (lower rate, massive audience)
  • Apple Music: Higher per-stream, but smaller audience
  • TIDAL: Highest rates, lowest audience

The Reality

Why Streaming Dominates

  • Spotify paid $11 billion in 2025
  • ~70% goes to rights holders
  • Massive total payouts, tiny individual earnings

Streaming Still Matters Because

  1. Discovery Engine: Song exposure is determined via algorithmically-curated playlists
  2. Funnel to Revenue: Integration with merch sites, ticketing platforms, sync placement.
  3. Global Distribution: Cheap to distribute + reaches a global audience

Structural Problems

  • Winner-takes-most: Labels have deals with platforms to push their biggest artists
  • Fraud/bot streams: AI-generated artists unfairly collect revenue
  • Oversupply: ~100K new tracks on Spotify every day

The Hard Truth

<10k streams = ~$0

100k streams = ~$300–$700

1M streams = ~$3k–$10k

Streaming alone is not sustainable income for most artists.
