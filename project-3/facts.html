The Tradeoff

Higher payout doesn't mean better.

Artists optimize for total revenue (streams × rate), not payout per stream.

  • Spotify: Reach + discovery (lower rate, massive audience)
  • Apple Music: Higher per-stream, but smaller audience
  • TIDAL: Highest rates, lowest audience

Critical Insight:

Streaming is a high-volume, low-margin economy where visibility is abundant but income is scarce.

The Reality

Why Streaming Dominates

  • Spotify paid $11 billion in 2025
  • ~70% goes to rights holders
  • Massive total payouts, tiny individual earnings

Streaming Still Matters Because

  1. Discovery Engine – Playlists & algorithms drive exposure
  2. Funnel to Revenue – Tours, merch, sync deals
  3. Global Distribution – Zero marginal cost, instant worldwide reach

Structural Problems

  • Winner-takes-most – Top artists capture disproportionate revenue
  • Fraud/bot streams – Artificial plays dilute revenue pool
  • Oversupply – Thousands of tracks uploaded daily

The Hard Truth

<10k streams = ~$0

100k streams = ~$300–$700

1M streams = ~$3k–$10k

Streaming alone is not sustainable income for most artists.
