When was the last time you felt alive?

My friends and I decided that 4am was too late to go to sleep, so we decided to stay up and watch the sunset on the beach. It was beautiful. Recently, standing somewhere high up with a view of the city just as the lights were coming on. The last time I stayed up too late working on a project and suddenly noticed it was 3 a.m. and I hadn’t checked the time once.
